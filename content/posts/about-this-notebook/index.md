This notebook brings together three kinds of writing: technical notes, reading and reflections, and travel journals. Older entries keep their original dates and language.

## Plain text, close at hand

Each entry lives in its own folder with its images. Markdown keeps the writing portable; Obsidian and MWeb can both edit the same files. Drafts stay out of the published website until they are ready.

## A little less work for the browser

Hugo builds the pages in advance. Images get explicit dimensions and responsive WebP versions. Mathematics becomes native MathML at build time, so reading an equation does not require downloading a mathematics JavaScript library.

For example, a recurrence for merge sort is

$$
T(n) = 2T\!\left(\frac{n}{2}\right) + \Theta(n)
$$

which gives a running time of \(O(n \log n)\).

## Room to grow

The structure is deliberately small. Tags connect related entries; series can collect notes from the same book or project. Search and RSS help make the notebook useful after the first visit.

The point is to leave more time for the next note.
